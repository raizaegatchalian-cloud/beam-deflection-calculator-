Quick Summary & Sign Conventions
Units & Sign Convention Length in m, Forces in kN (positive = downward ↓), Applied Moments in kN·m (positive = counterclockwise ↺), Modulus E in GPa, Moment of Inertia I in m⁴ / cm⁴ / mm⁴ / in⁴. Deflection y is negative (−) when sagging downward and positive (+) when lifting upward.
