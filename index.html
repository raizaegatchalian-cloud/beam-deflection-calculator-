Quick-Start Guide & Sign Conventions
Units & Sign Convention Length in m, Forces in kN (positive = downward ↓), Applied Moments in kN·m (positive = counterclockwise ↺), Modulus E in GPa, Moment of Inertia I in m⁴. Deflection y is reported in mm (negative = downward sag, positive = upward lift).
1. Double Integration Method Integrates the bending moment equation EI·y″(x) = M(x) twice and applies support boundary conditions to find closed-form slope and deflection equations.
2. Moment-Area Method Partitions the M(x)/EI curvature diagram into geometric areas Aᵢ with centroids x̄ᵢ, then uses reference tangents and tangential deviations to compute slopes and deflections.
3. Conjugate Beam Method Transforms real supports into conjugate supports (e.g., Fixed ↔ Free, Pin ↔ Pin), loads the beam with w*(x) = M(x)/EI, and reads real slope as conjugate shear V* and real deflection as conjugate moment M*.
